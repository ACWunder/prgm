Privacy Policy

For the app “PRGM: Party Games” and for this website. Last updated: November 27, 2026. The German version is the original; this is a translation.

In short

The app “PRGM: Party Games” (on the Home Screen: “PRGM”) does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party software. It needs no internet connection and sends nothing to me or to third parties. Only when you play Schocken with several phones do the phones nearby exchange game data directly with each other. This is described below under “Schocken with several phones”.

Personal data is only processed when you visit this website or send me an email. In addition, Apple processes data when you download the app from the App Store. Everything is described below.

The app

Names and game progress

Before a game, the group enters the names of the players. You decide which names you enter; nicknames are fine. The names, what you tap during the game, and the points and statistics of the round are kept only in the iPhone’s memory. The app does not store them permanently and does not send them anywhere, except to the other phones at the table in Schocken’s “Connect” mode. When the app is closed, they are deleted.

For the Jack and Queen cards, the group taps the names of the people the card applies to. The app does not ask for or store anyone’s gender. This selection, too, stays in memory only for the current game.

Language setting

The app remembers on the iPhone whether you chose English or German. This setting stays on the device, is not transmitted and is deleted together with the app.

Permissions

The app does not access the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. Only when you choose “Connect” in Schocken does iOS ask whether PRGM may find devices on your local network. If you decline, the rest of the app keeps working, including Schocken on one phone. You can change this permission at any time in iOS Settings under “Privacy & Security” → “Local Network”.

Schocken with several phones

In “Connect” mode the phones connect directly to each other using Multipeer Connectivity, a feature of iOS, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth nearby. There is no server and no internet connection involved, and none of this data reaches me.

Only what the game needs is transmitted: the four-digit table code, the names entered, the number of players and the course of the game, that is rolls and penalty points. The connection is encrypted. Phones nearby on which someone is currently looking for a table in PRGM can see open tables with the code and the name of the person who opened the table. Towards the other phones, your device only appears with a random identifier, not with its device name.

The data is kept only in the memory of the phones involved. It is deleted when the game ends or the app is closed.
