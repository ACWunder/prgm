PRGM: Party Games

Support and contact

PRGM: Party Games brings four party games to the iPhone: the card game Higher Lower with nine minigames, the dice game Schocken (on one phone or with several phones nearby), the flicking game Cap Shot and the card guessing game Window. The app is available in English and German. On the Home Screen it is called “PRGM”.

Frequently asked questions

Schocken can’t find the other phones. Every phone needs PRGM, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth turned on and permission for “Local Network” (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → PRGM). No internet connection is needed. Without a second phone you can play Schocken in “One phone” mode and pass the phone around.

How do I play a single minigame? Open Higher Lower, enter the names and tap “Minigames” at the top right.

How do I change the language? Use the flags at the top right of the start screen.
